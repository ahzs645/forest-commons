Completed mechanisms and validation are documented in `expansion-verification-2026-09-07.md` and the app README. “Implemented” means the stated teaching models are functional: it does not claim a global transport optimum, ecological calibration, institutional identity integration or public hosting. These are explicit scope limits, not hidden completion claims.
