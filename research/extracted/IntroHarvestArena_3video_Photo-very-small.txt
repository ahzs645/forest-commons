ppt/slides/slide18.xml
Quick review
3 steps
1) make initial plan 
2) select mill customers
3) adjust plan after follow-up
When satisfied, click   
Step
 1 – initial plan
A) Check production pace 
B) Adjust filter to show suitable contracts
C) Plan route between suitable harvesting sites.
Step 2 – selecting mill customers 
A) Production schedule (per team): click on site 
B) Production goals (per assortment): select  mill customer   
C) Cumulative volume: check cumulative production in relation to demand, per assortment, and per mill. 
Step 3 – adjusting plan after follow-up
Satisfied...?  Options:
	- change mill destination (click on «bucking») 
	- or delete site from schedule, and add a new one (refresh afterwards).
